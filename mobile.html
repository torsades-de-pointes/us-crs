About the score

US-CRS 2.0 estimates a candidate's risk of death or delisting for deterioration within 6 weeks without a transplant, from five labs and four devices. It is a proposed replacement for the 6-status ranking; it is not current OPTN policy.

Formula

LP = 7.45 − 0.837·albumin + 0.911·ln(bilirubin+1) − 0.00862·eGFR + 0.178·ln(NP+1) − 1.449·NT + 0.139·ln(NP+1)·NT − 0.07954·sodium − 0.43·LVAD + 0.757·IABP + 0.75·MFP + 1.676·aMCS

Score 50 if LP > −3.5457; score 1 if LP ≤ −9.4417; equal bins of 0.1228 between. 6-week risk ≈ 1 − (1 − h)6, h = logistic(LP); it over-predicts at the top of the scale.

Limitations

  • No hemodynamics, diagnosis, age or sex in the score.
  • Lab caps flatten the top of the scale.
  • NT-proBNP runs lower with obesity and in Black adults.
  • Device points depend on device access; not validated across social-risk groups.
  • Urgency only; says nothing about benefit after transplant.
